This repo predicts the next two weeks of MLB win-loss outcomes from each team's run differential.

Completed games come from the MLB Stats API. data/2026/completed_games.csv has 2469 finals from 2026-03-25 through 2026-10-28. The schedule was checked through 2026-11-20. Team files in data/2026/teams run from 2026-03-25 through 2026-10-28. New team rows added on this run: 0. Games skipped for a missing line, starter, or unknown team: 0. DraftKings was not called. The daily outcome script was not run. This does not price a bet.
