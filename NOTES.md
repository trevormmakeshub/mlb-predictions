This repo predicts the next two weeks of MLB win-loss outcomes from each team's run differential.

Completed games come from the MLB Stats API. data/2026/completed_games.csv has 2469 finals from 2026-03-25 through 2026-10-28. The schedule was checked through 2026-11-07. Team files in data/2026/teams include the same dates. No final was skipped for a missing line or starter. DraftKings was not called. The daily outcome script was not run; the 2026 season has no unfinished game on 2026-11-07. This does not price a bet.
